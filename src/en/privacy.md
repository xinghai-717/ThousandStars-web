# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 14, 2026

**Last Updated:** August 14, 2026

Welcome to "Thousand Stars Launcher" (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website and launcher.

Please read and understand this policy carefully before using our services. **If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our services.**

### 1. Information We Collect

The scope and manner of information we collect depend on the specific features you use:

*   **Information You Provide**: When you download resources through our website or launcher, we do not require you to register or provide personal information such as your name or email address.
*   **Information Automatically Collected**: Like many websites, our hosting provider (e.g., Cloudflare Pages) and the platforms we link to (e.g., GitHub) may automatically log certain standard information, including:
    *   **IP Address**: Used to identify your approximate geographic location and internet service provider.
    *   **Browser Type and Version**, **Operating System**: Used to optimize your access experience.
    *   **Access Time** and **Referrer Source**: Used for statistics and analytics.
    *   **Device Information**: If you download and use the launcher, the launcher itself may not actively collect information, but its runtime environment (e.g., the operating system) may generate standard log files.

> **Please Note**: We **do not** use third-party analytics tools such as Google Analytics, nor do we embed any SDKs in the launcher for the purpose of collecting user behavior data.

### 2. How We Use Information

The purposes for which we collect the above information are limited to:

*   **Providing Services**: Ensuring the normal operation of the website and download links, as well as the proper distribution of resources.
*   **Optimizing Experience**: Analyzing access data to improve website content and structure.
*   **Security Protection**: Monitoring and preventing fraud, abuse, and other security threats.

### 3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We take your privacy very seriously and **will not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties**.

We may share your information only in the following specific and necessary circumstances:

*   **With Third-Party Service Providers**: We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, providing services, or conducting analytics. For example, our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and our downloads rely on GitHub Releases. These service providers are only permitted to use your information for the purpose of providing services and are subject to strict confidentiality obligations.
*   **Legal Requirements**: We may be required to disclose your personal information if required by law, legal process, litigation, or government authorities.

### 4. Data Storage and Security

*   **Storage Location**: The data centers of our hosting providers (e.g., Cloudflare's global network) and file storage providers (e.g., GitHub) may involve cross-border data transfers. By using our services, you acknowledge and consent to this.
*   **Security Measures**: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, use, modification, damage, or loss. However, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

### 5. Your Rights

Under applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

1.  **Access**: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
2.  **Rectification**: Request that we update or correct information you believe is inaccurate.
3.  **Erasure**: Request that we delete your personal information (unless we have a legal basis to retain it).
4.  **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw your consent at any time for data processing based on your consent.

**How to exercise your rights?** Please contact us through the methods listed in the "Contact Us" section below. We will respond after verifying your identity.

### 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
